Entry for the make.fun bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run"
(`u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479`).

`main.ts` writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: line 1 explains an
echo to a 10-year-old through a thrown ball bouncing off a fence; line 2 says
honestly what the analogy maps (sound travelling out and returning) and what it
leaves out (echoes fade, a ball comes back just as hard).

Run by the grader as `node main.ts` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set. No network,
no randomness, no dependencies — the output is the same two lines every run.
