Entry for the make.fun bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run"
(`u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479`).

`main.ts` writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: line 1 explains a
shadow to a 10-year-old as a silent twin who copies every step but can never
pick anything up; line 2 says honestly what the analogy maps (the copying:
same shape, same moves, always stuck to your feet) and what it leaves out
(a twin is a real person, while a shadow is only blocked light with no body
at all).

Run by the grader as `node main.ts` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set. No network,
no randomness, no dependencies — the output is the same two lines every run.
